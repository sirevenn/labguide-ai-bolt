# LabGuide AI

> *Understand your lab test before you go.*<br>
> *Helping Ghanaians understand common medical lab tests in plain, everyday language before they visit a lab or clinic.*


LabGuide AI is a mobile-first web application that helps patients in Ghana understand laboratory tests in clear, everyday language. It explains why a test is performed, how to prepare, what to expect during sample collection, estimated costs, turnaround times, and where to access the test.

The goal is simple: **help patients arrive informed, prepared, and confident before visiting the laboratory.**

---

## Overview

Patients are often given laboratory request forms with little explanation. This can lead to unnecessary anxiety, incorrect preparation, repeated visits, and unexpected costs.

LabGuide AI bridges this information gap by providing patient-friendly laboratory test guidance alongside local laboratory information.

---

## Features

### Test Information

Each laboratory test includes:

- Purpose of the test
- Common names and abbreviations
- Preparation instructions
- Sample type
- Collection duration
- Estimated turnaround time
- Estimated price range
- NHIS coverage guidance

---

### Browse by Category

Tests are organized into practical patient-focused categories:

- Screening Tests
- Diabetes & Heart
- Women's Health
- General Checkup
- Baby Check
- Men's Health
- Antenatal
- Fertility

---

### Intelligent Search

Search tests using:

- Official test names
- Common names
- Abbreviations

Example:

```
Sugar test
FBS
Malaria
HIV
```

---

### Find a Laboratory

Locate laboratories offering a selected test, including:

- Facility name
- Area and city
- Opening hours

Initial coverage includes:

- Greater Accra
- Tema

---

### Compare Related Tests

Understand the differences between commonly confused laboratory tests.

Examples:

- Malaria RDT vs Blood Film
- HBsAg vs Hep C test

---

### Frequently Ordered Together

Discover laboratory tests that are commonly requested alongside the selected test.

---

### Save & Share

Patients can:

- Save tests for later
- Share test information with family members or healthcare professionals
- Copy a direct link to any test page

## Example Test Information

---

## Data Coverage

The application currently includes:

- Common laboratory investigations performed in Ghana
- Laboratory locations within Greater Accra and Tema
- Estimated patient pricing
- Patient preparation guidance
- Laboratory operating hours, contact and location

Coverage will expand to additional regions over time.

---

## Privacy

LabGuide AI is designed with patient privacy in mind.

- No account required
- No personal health information collected
- Saved items remain on the user's device
- Location is only requested when using **Find a Lab Near Me** and is never stored

---

## Medical Disclaimer

LabGuide AI is an educational resource only.

It does **not** diagnose medical conditions, interpret individual laboratory results, or replace advice from qualified healthcare professionals. Patients should always consult their doctor or laboratory for clinical decisions and confirmation of pricing or NHIS coverage.
